Title: Planted With Purpose
Opening Thought:
God never plants a seed without purpose. You are designed to carry something specific into the world.
Just like an apple seed cannot bear oranges, you cannot flourish in someone else’s calling.
Roots Before Fruit:
In business and in faith, unseen foundations matter most. A tree without deep roots cannot survive storms.
As a businessman, your “roots” are prayer, integrity, discipline, wise stewardship, and faithfulness in small things.
The Waiting Season:
Just as saplings take time before bearing fruit, your business and calling may take seasons of preparation before you see results.
Do not despise small beginnings. God values faithfulness more than speed.
Fruit for Others:
Trees don’t eat their own fruit. The profit, products, services, and impact of your business are meant to bless others — your employees, customers, family, and community.
Strength Through Giving:
The more fruit is plucked, the stronger the tree becomes. In the same way, generosity and service enlarge your capacity.
A businessman who gives becomes more resilient, not less.
Closing Reflection:
Ask yourself:
- Am I rooted deeply enough to withstand storms?
- Am I patient in seasons of waiting?
- Am I producing fruit that feeds others, not just myself?
