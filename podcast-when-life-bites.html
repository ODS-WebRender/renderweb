When Life Bites

For survivors navigating the crises that reshape us—trauma, grief, betrayal, and loss. This is your companion through the hardest seasons. A place where pain meets faith, where real stories meet biblical hope, and where healing becomes possible.

✓

Real Stories — Honest accounts from those who've survived

✓

Why This Podcast?

💔

You're Not Alone

When life falls apart, isolation multiplies the pain. Real voices share real journeys—proof that healing is possible.

📖

Scripture-Rooted

Ancient wisdom for modern pain. We explore biblical truths—from Psalm 23 to 1 Peter to Job—that sustain us through crisis.

🌱

Path Forward

Moving beyond survival mode to thriving. Resources for forgiveness, reconciliation, and stepping into purpose after tragedy.

Sexual abuse by a trusted family member or friend. Navigating shock, rage, justice, and the long path to protecting your child and healing the wound.

Episode 2

The Mask Falls

Marital deception and betrayal

🎭

Audio Player

~31 minutes

The spouse who is not who you thought they were. Hidden identity, chronic infidelity, a baby that isn't yours. Navigating truth, accountability, and choosing your next step.

Scripture Focus: Jeremiah 17:9-10

Topics: Betrayal, truth, boundaries, restoration or separation

Episode 3

Love Without Agenda

Your child's LGBTQ+ identity

🏳️‍🌈

Audio Player

~26 minutes

Your son or daughter declares they are gay. Or they identify as transgender. As a Christian parent, how do you respond with grace, listen without judgment, and keep love as the loudest voice?

Scripture Focus: 1 John 4:7-8

Topics: Parental identity, faith crisis, unconditional love, listening

Episode 4

The Bottom Line

📉

Audio Player

~30 minutes

Your business has collapsed. You've lost everything. And now the world measures your worth by what you no longer have. Finding identity beyond the balance sheet, and hope beyond shame.

Scripture Focus: Habakkuk 3:17-18

Topics: Financial loss, identity, shame, defiant hope
