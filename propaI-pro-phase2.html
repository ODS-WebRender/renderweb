Frequently Asked Questions

Does PropAI integrate with my existing property management software?

Yes. Professional and Enterprise plans include API access and integrations with major PMS platforms (Rent Manager, AppFolio, CoreLogic, etc.). Our team handles the setup. For other systems, custom integrations are available.

How accurate is the dynamic pricing model?

Our model analyzes 50K+ listings across 15 markets and has achieved 91% accuracy in predicting market rent ranges. However, you always make the final pricing decision—PropAI‑Pro is an advisory tool, not autonomous.

What if a tenant disputes something from the AI-generated lease?

You review and customize every lease before sending to tenants. PropAI‑Pro just generates the first draft from your templates, filling in property details and tenant info. All legal responsibility remains yours.

How does the trial work? Can I go month-to-month after?

Starter tier includes a 14-day free trial with all features unlocked. After trial, billing starts. You can cancel anytime or switch to annual billing for a 15% discount. Month-to-month available for all plans.

Is tenant data encrypted and compliant with privacy laws?

All tenant data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We comply with POPIA (South Africa), GDPR (EU), and CCPA (US). Enterprise plans include SOC 2 Type 2 certification and regular security audits.

What's the implementation timeline for a large property company?

For Professional tier: 2–3 weeks for onboarding. For Enterprise: 6–8 weeks (includes data migration, API integrations, staff training, testing). We assign a dedicated project manager.

Can I export my data if I leave PropAI‑Pro?

Absolutely. You own your data. Professional and Enterprise plans include full data export (CSV, JSON). We can help with migration to another system. Starter tier also includes export on request.
