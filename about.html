The Story Behind the 9Count

A journey from rock bottom to building tools for the fighters

The Story

For years, I stood at the helm of a successful construction company. We were growing, building, and thriving—until, without warning, the foundations gave way. In a sudden turn of events, a single contract in a coastal town became the catalyst for a total collapse. I found myself at rock bottom, facing a level of loss that felt final.

During the quiet of that recovery, I began researching business systems for Back Office Management and BPO outsourcing. That search led me to a podcasting bootcamp. As a novice in the space, I quickly saw the gap: most creators have the voice, but they lack the technical "wherewithal" to manage the workflow.

What started as a personal dashboard to solve my own problems evolved into a mission to empower others. I realized that my decades of business experience combined with new technology could help others build their own platforms without the technical overwhelm.

The 9Count Philosophy

In boxing, the 9-count is the last moment of hope. It is the moment when the crowd has gone quiet, the lights feel dim, and everyone assumes the fight is over. But at the count of nine, I chose to get up. I went back to my corner, wiped away the blood, and came back swinging.

In my world, a loss is only official at the count of ten. We haven't reached ten yet. This resilience is the heartbeat of my two brands:

Old Dog Systems

A defiant nod to the phrase "you can't teach an old dog new tricks". I am proving that experience is the best foundation for innovation as I master coding and application development.

9Count Systems

A tribute to the grit it takes to stand up when the world expects you to stay down.

What We Build

Our flagship project, Rough Diamond Studio, is a specialized dashboard designed to take the friction out of audio creation. It is currently in development and moving toward its initial testing phases. Behind it, a suite of modular systems for small businesses is being crafted—each designed to streamline operations for the "operators" of the world.

We don't just build software; we build tools for the fighters. We create systems and content for those who have been on the canvas, dusted themselves off, and are ready to come back swinging.
