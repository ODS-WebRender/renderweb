TTS Engine & Voice Synthesis

Production-quality synthesizer with multi-voice and emotion support.

View TTS Engine Details

VoiceSynthesizer: Direct and asynchronous synthesis returning 48kHz, 16-bit stereo PCM. Supports multiple voices and emotions with embedding extraction for voice cloning.

  • Blocking and async synth APIs (progress callbacks)
  • Pre-defined voices: Morgan, Emma, James (production-calibrated)
  • Emotion system: Neutral, Happy, Sad, Angry, Surprised
  • Speaker embedding extraction for personal voice profiles
Example: voiceSynthesizer->synthesize(voice, text, emotion)
UI Framework & Controls

Full UI panel with composition, narration, and post-production tabs, plus prosody sliders and control buttons.

  • Text editor, voice & emotion combo boxes, pitch/speed/emphasis/intensity sliders
  • Synthesize / Preview / Insert controls with real-time status and progress
  • Signal/slot wiring for responsive interactions and real-time feedback
Signal/Slot & Integration Hooks

All major controls emit signals for immediate UI updates and background tasks.

  • Slider valueChanged → label updates (pitch, speed, emphasis, intensity)
  • Button handlers validate state and manage progress display
  • Ready for Phase 2C real integration with synthesis and audio engine

Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I import existing podcasts into Studio?

Yes. We support RSS imports and bulk upload from your existing host. During onboarding, our team guides the migration. You keep all your podcast data—we just organize and enhance it within Studio.

How does the AI show notes feature work?

Upload your episode audio or transcript. Our AI automatically generates timestamped show notes, key takeaways, and guest bios. You can edit everything before publishing. Average generation time: 5 minutes for a 60-minute episode.

What platforms can I publish to directly?

Direct integrations: Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube, LinkedIn. One-click publishing syncs metadata, artwork, and descriptions. For other platforms, export your RSS feed or media files.

Is my audio data secure and private?

All files are encrypted in transit and at rest. You own 100% of your content—we never monetize or share your audio. Backups are automated daily. Data deletion is permanent upon request.

Can multiple people edit the same episode?

Yes. Creator and Studio Pro plans include team workspaces with role-based permissions. See who's editing in real-time, leave comments, and control who can publish. Perfect for production teams.
