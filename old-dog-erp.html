Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Old Dog ERP truly free if we self-host?

Yes. You download the open-source code, run it on your own servers (Linux, Windows, macOS, cloud VMs), and pay nothing. You only pay for hosting infrastructure and any custom development or support services you need.

How long does implementation typically take?

For smaller deployments (single-location, basic setup): 2–4 weeks. For larger enterprises with complex workflows and third-party integrations: 8–16 weeks. Our implementation team works with you to prioritize modules and create a phased rollout plan.

Can we integrate Old Dog ERP with our existing accounting software?

Yes. Old Dog ERP has REST APIs and native integrations with QuickBooks, Xero, Sage, and Wave. For other systems, custom middleware can be built. Our team assesses your stack during the discovery phase and recommends integration approaches.

Does Old Dog ERP meet compliance requirements (GAAP, IFRS, tax laws)?

The core GL engine is GAAP/IFRS compliant. Tax and regulatory compliance features vary by region. Managed Cloud and Enterprise Support customers get compliance audits and localization support. Consult our sales team for your specific jurisdiction.

How does data security work if we're self-hosting?

You control the infrastructure and security. Old Dog ERP uses industry-standard encryption (AES-256 at rest, TLS in transit), role-based access control, and audit logging. We provide security hardening guides and best practices documentation.

Can we customize workflows and fields to our business needs?

Absolutely. No-code customization is available (custom fields, workflow rules, report builders). For deeper changes, the open-source architecture allows custom development. Our ecosystem has certified developers and consultants who specialize in Old Dog ERP implementations.

What's the community like? How active is development?

Active. 2,000+ production deployments, 500+ community members on Slack, and regular monthly releases with new features and security updates. GitHub repository has 1.2K+ contributors. You can see the full roadmap and contribute.
