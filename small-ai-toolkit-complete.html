Frequently Asked Questions

How long does implementation take?

Pilot deployments typically go live within 2 weeks of contract signing. Our team handles all hardware setup and worker training. For Growth Plan, scaling to additional sites takes 1–2 weeks per site depending on network infrastructure.

What if we don't have modern equipment?

We supply IoT sensors, cameras, and tablets as part of the Pilot. Monthly hardware costs (depreciation model) are included in pricing. No upfront equipment purchase needed—deduct from ROI achieved.

How accurate is the safety AI? Can it miss things?

Our models are trained on 50K+ site hours and achieve 94% accuracy on PPE detection. We're transparent: no AI replaces your safety officers. CPM-AI is an always-on extra set of eyes that alerts humans to investigate. Your team makes final safety decisions.

Do you offer data guarantees or SLAs?

Yes. Growth and Enterprise plans include 99.5% uptime SLA backed by redundant South Africa hosting. All site data owned by you—we provide export APIs. Compliance with POPIA and construction safety standards verified quarterly.

What's the onboarding process for my crew?

We run a 2-hour onsite training session with your safety manager and 2–3 site supervisors. Materials translated into local languages. Ongoing video tutorials available in-app. We're available via Slack for questions the first 2 weeks.
